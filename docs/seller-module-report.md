# Seller Portal Enhancement Report

This report documents the enhancements made to the Seller Console.

---

## 1. Seller Verification & KYC
- **Business Verification**: Collects and validates PAN, GST, and bank verification records.
- **KYC Management**: Documents are uploaded and marked for Admin review before listing products.

---

## 2. Advanced Analytics Dashboard
- **Financial Analytics**: Displays Gross Revenue, Commissions, Net Profit margins, and Refund Rates.
- **Campaign Manager**: Allows sellers to launch coupon codes, promotions, and flash sales.
- **Performance Scorecards**: Tracks late shipment rate, fulfillment rate, and cancellation rate.
